Pet Insurance in India

BTW IMF Advisory Team28 April 2026

Pet Insurance in India: complete guide to cost, coverage and benefits (2026). Pet insurance is becoming essential for modern pet owners as veterinary costs continue to rise. Whether it is a dog greeting you at the door or a cat curling up beside you, that companionship comes with responsibility — especially around healthcare.

A routine vet visit may seem affordable, but emergencies, surgeries and long-term treatments can quickly become expensive. That is where a pet insurance policy helps: it lets you manage these costs without stress.

Why pet insurance matters

Medical emergencies do not come with a warning. A minor infection, an accident or a surgery can lead to unexpected bills. Having a policy in place means you do not have to compromise on your pet’s treatment, and you can focus on recovery instead of cost.

Some plans also include liability cover — if your pet accidentally injures someone or damages property, those costs may be covered too. It is a benefit many owners overlook.

What does it cover?

Most plans available in India include a mix of the following:

  • Illness treatment — common diseases, infections and conditions that need medical attention.
  • Accidents — injuries such as fractures or wounds and the treatment they need.
  • Surgery and hospitalisation — from minor procedures to major surgery, including vet fees, ICU charges and medicines.
  • Loss or theft — some policies pay compensation if your pet is lost or stolen.
  • Death benefit — financial compensation to the owner in unfortunate situations.

What is not covered?

Understanding the exclusions is just as important:

  • Pre-existing illnesses
  • Cosmetic or non-essential procedures
  • Breeding or pregnancy-related costs
  • Negligence or improper care
  • Routine vaccinations (in many cases)

Always read the fine print before choosing a policy.

Popular insurance providers

Well-known companies offering pet-related policies in India include:

  • New India Assurance — government-backed and budget-friendly
  • Bajaj Allianz General Insurance — comprehensive plans with good service
  • Future Generali India Insurance — flexible coverage options
  • Digit Insurance — easy online process and transparent pricing
  • Oriental Insurance Company — reliable and affordable

How much does it cost?

The premium depends on a few key factors: the breed (pedigree dogs usually cost more), the age of the pet, its health condition and the coverage amount you choose.

Typical price range:

  • Dogs: ₹2,000 – ₹10,000 per year
  • Cats: ₹1,500 – ₹6,000 per year

Set against the cost of emergency treatment, this is quite reasonable.

Is pet insurance in India worth it?

In cities like Mumbai, Pune and Bangalore, veterinary expenses can be high, and a single surgery may cost more than an annual premium. Buying a policy early — while your pet is young — usually means better coverage at a lower cost, and helps you avoid complications related to pre-existing conditions later.

Conclusion

Choosing the right pet insurance plan helps secure your pet’s future while giving you peace of mind. A well-chosen plan ensures timely treatment without putting pressure on your budget. In simple terms, it is not just an expense — it is a safety net for your pet’s health and your financial stability.

Frequently asked questions

Is pet insurance in India worth it?

Yes — it helps manage rising veterinary costs and provides financial security when your pet needs surgery, hospitalisation or emergency care.

What is the cost of pet insurance in India?

It usually ranges between about ₹2,000 and ₹10,000 per year for dogs and ₹1,500 to ₹6,000 per year for cats, depending on breed, age, health and the coverage amount.
