The process

How claim support works

Four steps, one dedicated manager — so nothing falls through the cracks.

Tell us what happened

Call, WhatsApp or submit the form below. For a planned hospitalisation or a motor repair, reach us before you proceed; for an emergency or accident, within 24 hours.

We prepare the file

Your claim manager confirms the policy covers the event, gives you the exact document list, and vets every paper before submission — incomplete or wrong paperwork is the main reason claims stall.

Filing & follow-up

We submit to the insurer or TPA, answer their queries, arrange the surveyor visit where one is needed, and chase every pending step so it doesn’t go quiet.

Settlement

Cashless claims are paid to the hospital or garage; reimbursement claims are credited to your account. We check the amount against your policy and flag any shortfall or deduction.

Be ready

Documents you’ll usually need

A head start on the two most common claim types. Your claim manager gives you the exact list for your case.

For a health claim

  • Signed claim form
  • Policy copy / health e-card and photo ID
  • Doctor’s prescription and hospital discharge summary
  • Original hospital bills, receipts and pharmacy invoices
  • Diagnostic and investigation reports
  • FIR / MLC copy (for an accident)
  • Cancelled cheque or bank details of the proposer

For a motor claim

  • Claim form and policy copy
  • Copy of RC and the driving licence
  • FIR (for theft, third-party injury or a major accident)
  • Repair estimate, then the final tax invoice after repair
  • Photographs of the damage
  • Both keys, Form 28/29/30 and loan NOC (for theft / total loss)

FAQs

Claims — frequently asked questions

Does BTW IMF approve or reject my claim?

No. BTW IMF is an IRDAI-licensed intermediary and facilitator, not a surveyor or the insurer. We prepare and pursue your claim, but the decision to approve, partly settle or reject it rests entirely with the insurance company under IRDAI regulations.

Is claim assistance free?

Yes. Claim support is part of the service for every policy BTW IMF places for you — there is no separate fee, and you pay the same premium as buying direct.

What should I do first when something happens?

Contact us (or the insurer’s helpline) as early as possible — before a planned hospitalisation or motor repair, and within 24 hours of an emergency or accident. Early intimation is a policy condition and prevents avoidable rejections.

My claim was rejected — can you still help?

Yes. We review the rejection reason against your policy wording, help you supply anything that was missing, and represent your case for reconsideration, then escalation to the insurer’s grievance cell and, if needed, the Insurance Ombudsman.

Can you help with a policy I didn’t buy through BTW IMF?

Our full claim-management service is for policies we hold for you. For other policies we can give general guidance, but we cannot act as your intermediary with that insurer.

What is a surveyor, and will I need one?

For most motor, property and marine claims above a small amount, the insurer appoints an independent IRDAI-licensed surveyor to assess the loss. We coordinate the visit; the surveyor’s assessment — not ours — informs the settlement.

How long does a claim take?

A cashless health pre-authorisation is often approved within hours. Reimbursement and motor claims usually settle in 7–21 days after complete documents; property and marine claims take longer due to the survey. IRDAI requires insurers to settle within 30 days of receiving the last required document, or pay interest.
