Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, share and protect your personal information when you use this website or our insurance and investment advisory services.

Effective date: 4 September 2026. This policy applies to www.btwimf.com and the services of BTW Financial Services & IMF Pvt Ltd.

Data collection

We collect personal and sensitive information that is necessary to deliver our insurance advisory services effectively. This includes your full name, contact number, email address, residential or correspondence address, date of birth, identity documents (such as PAN, Aadhaar or passport), health and financial information, insurance preferences and transaction details. This data may be collected through online forms, direct communication, or when you engage with our services via this website or our support channels.

Purpose of use

The information we collect is used strictly for legitimate business purposes. These include recommending suitable insurance products, processing policy applications, facilitating claim submissions, issuing documentation, providing policy-related updates, and ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations. With your permission, we may also use your data to inform you about relevant products, renewal reminders or special offers.

Data sharing

Your personal data is shared only when necessary, and always in a secure and responsible manner. We may share your information with insurance companies to issue policies or assist with claims, with regulators or government authorities as required by law, and with authorised third-party vendors (such as payment gateways, CRM providers or technical support services) who help us operate. These partners are bound by confidentiality and data-protection agreements. We never sell or rent your personal information to any third party.

Data protection

We are committed to securing your data using industry-standard security technologies and best practices. This website is protected with SSL encryption, and we use firewalls, role-based access and secure servers to prevent unauthorised access, misuse or alteration of your data. We also conduct regular security reviews and ensure that staff handling your information are trained in data-protection practices.

Working with service providers

To support the smooth operation of our business and website, we may engage trusted third-party service providers for specific tasks such as distributing newsletters, conducting surveys, managing communications or improving user experience. Your information may be shared with these providers solely to carry out those services on our behalf, limited in scope and governed by strict confidentiality.

Cookies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember your preferences and analyse site traffic. These cookies do not collect personal data unless you explicitly provide it. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may affect certain functionality.

Third-party links

This website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms. These operate independently of us and have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the content, accuracy or practices of such websites, and we encourage you to review their privacy terms before engaging with them.

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be revised from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, business practices or technology. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Please review this policy periodically to stay informed about how your data is protected.

Your acceptance

By using this website you agree to and accept the terms of this Privacy Policy. Your continued use of the site after any updates will be treated as acceptance of those changes. For any question about your data, or to request a change to the information we hold, contact us at info@btwimf.com.
