In short

  • Eligible pets: dogs and cats, typically from 8 weeks (or 3 months) up to 8–10 years of age at entry.
  • What it pays: reimbursement of covered vet costs up to a chosen sum insured (commonly ₹20,000 to ₹2–3 lakh a year).
  • Waiting periods: a short wait for accident cover (a few days) and a longer wait for illness (about 15–30 days), with specific illnesses waiting longer.
  • Co-payment and per-condition sub-limits are common — you share a percentage of each claim.
  • Enrol young: conditions that appear before cover starts become pre-existing and are excluded.

What is Pet Insurance in India?

Pet insurance is an indemnity health cover for a companion animal. You register the pet (with age, breed and often a vet health certificate or microchip), choose an annual sum insured, and the insurer reimburses eligible treatment costs during the year — subject to waiting periods, any co-payment and sub-limits.

It is a newer line in India, offered by a small number of general insurers, and the wordings vary more than in mature markets. Beyond vet bills, plans commonly bundle third-party liability (valuable if your dog bites someone or causes an accident), and offer add-ons for theft, loss or straying, mortality (a benefit if the pet dies from accident or covered illness), and OPD/wellness for vaccinations and routine care. Coverage

What pet insurance covers — and what it doesn’t

A quick side-by-side so you know exactly where the policy helps.

Typically covered

  • Veterinary treatment for accidental injury — consultation, diagnostics, medication, surgery
  • Treatment for illness and disease after the illness waiting period
  • Hospitalisation and day-care surgical procedures at a registered veterinary hospital
  • Third-party liability — legal liability if your pet injures a person or damages their property
  • Emergency boarding / minding costs if you are hospitalised (on some plans)
  • Optional — theft, loss and straying, with advertising and reward costs
  • Optional — mortality / death benefit, and OPD & wellness (vaccination, deworming, health checks)

Usually excluded

  • Pre-existing conditions and anything showing symptoms before or during the waiting period
  • Routine and preventive care unless an OPD/wellness add-on is taken
  • Elective procedures — cosmetic, behavioural, grooming, dental scaling
  • Pregnancy, breeding, whelping and related complications (unless specifically covered)
  • Treatment for illnesses preventable by vaccination the pet had not received
  • Deliberate neglect, illegal ownership, racing or fighting, and commercial use
  • Costs above the annual sum insured, a per-condition sub-limit, or the co-payment share

Exact inclusions and exclusions vary by insurer and plan — your BTW IMF advisor walks you through the wording before you buy.

Options

Types of pet insurance

Plans differ mainly by what they bundle beyond vet bills.

Accident-only plan

Covers vet costs from accidental injury only, with a short waiting period. The lowest-cost entry point.

  • Cheapest premium
  • Short waiting period
  • No illness cover

Accident + illness plan

The standard comprehensive plan — accident and illness treatment up to the annual sum insured, plus third-party liability.

  • Covers vet bills broadly
  • Liability included
  • Choose the sum insured

Comprehensive + add-ons

Accident + illness plus theft/straying, mortality benefit and OPD/wellness for routine care.

  • Covers loss and death
  • Vaccination and check-ups
  • Higher premium

Third-party liability only

Covers just the legal liability if your pet injures someone or damages property — no vet cover.

  • Focused liability protection
  • Low premium
  • Often a society/landlord requirement

How to choose the right pet insurance

  1. Match the cover to the risk. Work out the worst realistic loss — a long hospital stay, a total-loss claim, an income gap — and buy enough pet insurance to absorb it.
  2. Compare like for like. Premium is only meaningful next to the sum insured, sub-limits, waiting periods and exclusions. We line these up side by side.
  3. Check the insurer, not just the plan. Claim-settlement record, turnaround time and how the insurer treats disclosure disputes matter as much as price.
  4. Add only the riders you'll use. A few add-ons sharply improve real payouts; the rest just raise the premium.

Who needs pet insurance?

Dog and cat owners who would find a large, unexpected vet bill hard to absorb — emergency surgery, a fracture repair, or treatment for a chronic illness can run into tens of thousands of rupees. Owners of larger or guarding breeds particularly benefit from the third-party liability element. Enrolling while the pet is young and healthy secures the widest cover, since later-appearing conditions are excluded as pre-existing.

What affects your premium

  • The pet's species, breed and age at enrolment
  • Annual sum insured and any per-condition sub-limits
  • Co-payment percentage you accept
  • Add-ons — theft/straying, mortality, OPD & wellness
  • Vaccination and neutering status, and prior medical history
  • City of residence

Useful add-ons & riders

Riders widen pet insurance for a small extra premium. The ones worth considering:

  • Third-party liability higher limit
  • Theft, loss and straying cover with advertising and reward costs
  • Mortality / death benefit (accident and covered illness)
  • OPD and wellness — vaccination, deworming, annual health check
  • Terminal-illness and end-of-life (humane euthanasia) costs
  • Emergency pet-minding or boarding if the owner is hospitalised
FAQs

Pet Insurance in India — frequently asked questions

Is pet insurance available in India?

Yes. A small but growing number of general insurers offer pet insurance for dogs and cats, covering vet bills for accident and illness along with third-party liability and optional theft, straying and mortality benefits. Wordings and limits vary widely between insurers, so comparison matters.

What does pet insurance cover?

Core cover reimburses veterinary treatment for accidental injury and illness — consultations, diagnostics, surgery and hospitalisation — up to an annual sum insured. Most plans also include third-party liability if your pet injures someone or damages property. Routine care, pre-existing conditions and elective procedures are excluded unless specifically added.

At what age can I insure my dog or cat?

Most insurers accept pets from about 8 weeks or 3 months old, up to a maximum entry age of 8–10 years depending on the breed. Enrolling while the pet is young and healthy is important, because any condition that appears before cover starts is treated as pre-existing and excluded permanently.

Are pre-existing conditions covered?

No. Any illness or injury that existed, or showed symptoms, before the policy started or during its waiting period is excluded. This is why early enrolment matters and why you should renew without a break — a lapse can reset the position and turn ongoing conditions into pre-existing ones.

Does pet insurance cover vaccinations and routine check-ups?

Not under the core cover, which is for treatment of illness and injury. Some insurers offer an optional OPD or wellness add-on that reimburses vaccinations, deworming and an annual health check up to a small sub-limit.

How are pet insurance claims paid?

Almost all pet claims in India are on a reimbursement basis: you pay the vet, then submit the claim form, treatment notes, reports and original bills. The insurer assesses them against the policy and pays the eligible amount — less any co-payment and sub-limit — into your bank account.
