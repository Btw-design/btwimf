The team

Who you’ll actually deal with

Behind every BTW IMF policy is a small, specialised team — not a call-centre queue.

Licensed insurance advisors

IRDAI-certified advisors who compare 15+ insurers, explain the trade-offs in plain language, and recommend what actually fits your need — never what pays the most commission.

Dedicated claim managers

One person owns your claim from intimation to settlement — preparing the file, coordinating with the insurer or TPA, and chasing every pending step until the money is paid.

Relationship managers

Your single point of contact for renewals, endorsements, nominee changes and address updates — so servicing a policy never means starting from scratch.

Operations & compliance

The team that keeps every policy IRDAI-compliant, every document in order, and every disclosure accurate — the quiet work that makes claims pay later.

How we work

What every team member is held to

Clients first, always

Every recommendation starts from what the client needs, not what we’d earn. It’s the founder’s philosophy and the whole team’s standard.

Unbiased by design

As an Insurance Marketing Firm we place policies from many insurers — so our advice compares the market rather than selling one brand.

Support for the life of the policy

Renewals, endorsements, health check-ups and claims — handled for you, free, for as long as you hold the policy.

At your doorstep

Free consultations at your home or office across Mumbai and Pune. Advice first, paperwork later.
